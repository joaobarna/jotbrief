Extension Privacy Policy

SaidKeep: names in Google Meet. Last updated: October 3, 2026.

In short: the extension sends no data to the developer's servers or to any third party. The names of who is speaking in Google Meet go only to the SaidKeep app installed on your own computer.

What the extension does

While you are in a Google Meet call, the extension watches the page to see which participant is speaking and tells the SaidKeep (Windows) app running on your computer. That way the voices in the transcript get the speaker's name. This is the extension's only purpose.

What data it accesses

  • Participants' display names and the "speaking" indicator, which already appear on the Google Meet page. This happens only on meet.google.com and only while the page is open.
  • The extension does not read, record or send audio, video, chat messages, e-mails or the content of the conversation.

Where the data goes

  • Only to http://127.0.0.1:47821, which is the SaidKeep app running on your computer. The app stores these notices in the meeting folder, also on your computer.
  • There is no developer server, usage analytics, advertising, sale or sharing of data with third parties.
  • The extension does not download or run remote code.

What is stored

Only one local preference: show or hide the badge in Google Meet (in chrome.storage.local).

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionPurpose
Access to meet.google.comRead the names and the speaking indicator on the call page.
Access to http://127.0.0.1:47821Talk to the SaidKeep app on your computer.
alarmsPeriodically check whether the app is recording, to show the red circle on the icon.
storageRemember your choice to show or hide the badge.

Your controls

Uninstalling the extension deletes the preference. Meetings and notices recorded by the app stay on your computer, in the Documents\SaidKeep folder, and you can delete them at any time.

Important notices

  • Recording and transcribing meetings requires the participants' consent. Let them know before you start.
  • This extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google. "Google Meet" is a trademark of Google LLC.
